## 5. Google Business Profile kit (how you rank locally)

**Why it matters most right now:** for "ai agent development company in india", Google shows a map pack whose three listings have only **24, 7 and 5 reviews**. A complete, verified profile with 15 to 25 genuine reviews can compete there long before the website ranks organically.

**Eligibility, read this first.** Google only allows a profile for a business that meets customers in person during its posted hours, at a real staffed address. A virtual office or an unstaffed coworking desk isn't allowed and can get the profile suspended. If clients can meet you at a real Bengaluru office, list it. If you only work remotely, set up as a service-area business, hide the address, and set the service area to Bengaluru. Verification is usually by video.

**Set up at business.google.com:**

- **Business name:** `WhiteGuava`, exactly. Don't add keywords like "WhiteGuava AI Development Company". Google suspends profiles for that, and it's the most common reason listings disappear.
- **Primary category:** `Software company`.
- **Secondary categories:** `Computer consultant` and `Business management consultant`. Also type "artificial intelligence" into the category box, and if Google offers an AI-specific category, add it. Pick only from Google's list.
- **Website:** `https://www.thewhiteguava.in/`
- **Phone:** one number you will use everywhere (site, GBP, directories).
- **Social profiles:** add Instagram `https://www.instagram.com/thewhiteguava.in/`, plus LinkedIn, X and Facebook.
- **Description (725 of 750 characters, ready to paste):**

  > WhiteGuava is an AI software development company in Bengaluru, Karnataka. We build AI agents, AI automation, WhatsApp AI chatbots, document AI and custom business software for companies in India and worldwide. We also deploy the Guava Product Suite: CRM, ERP, HR, LMS, helpdesk, BI dashboards, project management, loan management and website builder applications that run on your own cloud with an AI assistant built in. You pay a one-time setup fee and a small monthly server cost instead of per-user subscriptions, and you own the software and your data. Our team handles architecture, integration with your existing systems, deployment and support. Tell us the problem you want solved and we will scope a working solution.

- **Services** (add each one, linking to its page): AI Agent Development · AI Automation · WhatsApp AI Chatbots · Document AI · AI Software Development · Data & Analytics · Cloud & Deployment.
- **Products:** add all 10 Guava products (GuavaCRM, GuavaERP, GuavaHR, GuavaLearn, GuavaInsights, GuavaDesk, GuavaBuilder, GuavaLend, GuavaPlan, GuavaFramework), each with a one-line description and its `/blog/one-time-payment-*` link. Use the footer list in `site.ts` as the source.
- **Photos:** logo, cover, the office exterior and interior with signage, the team at work, and product screenshots. Profiles with real photos get noticeably more engagement.
- **Posts:** one a week. Reuse the Instagram calendar: "Stop renting your CRM", "What 10 CRM seats really cost", the WhatsApp AI agent demo, "Guava suite: 10 tools you rent vs own".
- **Reviews:** ask every past and current client. Don't offer anything in return and don't write them yourself, since both break Google's policy. Reply to every review. Message to send:

  > Hi {name}, thanks again for working with WhiteGuava on {project}. If you have two minutes, a short Google review would help other businesses find us: {review link}. Thank you!

**What I need from you to finish local SEO in the code:** the street address and pincode (or confirmation you're service-area only), the phone number, opening hours, and the profile's map link once it's verified. With those, I'll add `streetAddress`, `postalCode`, `telephone`, `geo`, `openingHours` and `hasMap` to the Organization schema and show the address in the footer, so every listing matches the website exactly.

## 8. Speed plan (do after the deploy, then re-test)

1. **Homepage JavaScript** is the main problem: 4 s of script work and 2.4 s of blocked main thread on a mid-range phone. The opening story sequence, GSAP, framer-motion and the globe all load up front. Load below-the-fold sections with `next/dynamic`, and keep heavy effects off phones.
2. **Clarity**, from the new GitHub commits, loads early, adds third-party cookies (the Best Practices score is 79) and runs a 250 ms task at startup. Start it after the page has loaded instead.
3. **HTML weight:** the homepage HTML is 337 KB, mostly the React data payload. Moving static sections to server components shrinks it.
4. Re-test at https://pagespeed.web.dev after each change. Google's free API quota was used up today, so these numbers come from Lighthouse run locally, which is the same engine.
